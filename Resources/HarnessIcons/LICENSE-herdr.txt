herdr.svg and herdr-color.svg are drawn from the Herdr logo
(https://github.com/herdrdev/herdr, commit 3d9d2b18dab139ba226ebc5a1c9a9f2c9c3ee4df, assets/logo.svg).
The path is unchanged; the view box is cropped to the head, herdr.svg fills
it with currentColor, and herdr-color.svg keeps the logo's two colors on a
rounded square.
